IMAGES FOR AID-CARE-EVERYWHERE
==============================

Put every image in this "images" folder. Use lowercase file names with
no spaces (for example crisis-1.jpg, not "Crisis 1.JPG").

FILE NAME              WHERE IT APPEARS                       SIZE / SHAPE
---------------------  -------------------------------------  -------------------------
logo.png (or .svg)     Header of every page                   square, at least 128x128 px
favicon.svg            Browser tab icon (placeholder included) square
crisis-1.jpg           Home page poster 1 + blurred background landscape, about 1200x800 px
crisis-2.jpg           Home page poster 2 + blurred background landscape, about 1200x800 px
crisis-3.jpg           Home page poster 3 + blurred background landscape, about 1200x800 px
crisis-4.jpg           Home page poster 4 + blurred background landscape, about 1200x800 px
crisis-5.jpg           Home page poster 5 + blurred background landscape, about 1200x800 px
crisis-1-poster.jpg    Crisis section poster (Placeholder 1)  wide, at least 1600x1000 px
team-1.jpg             About Us, team card 1                  square, about 300x300 px
team-2.jpg             About Us, team card 2                  square, about 300x300 px
team-3.jpg             About Us, team card 3                  square, about 300x300 px

TIPS
- Keep each photo under about 300 KB so pages load quickly
  (free tools such as squoosh.app can shrink them).
- Only use photos you are allowed to use: your own photos, photos an
  organization has given you permission to use, or openly licensed photos
  (for example Creative Commons) with the credit they ask for.
- Always fill in the credit line ("Photo: ...") under each crisis photo.
- Choose dignified photos that show people as active and resilient.
  Avoid graphic images and photos of identifiable children in distress.
- Every image needs alt text: a short description of what it shows,
  e.g. alt="Aid workers unloading food sacks from a truck".
