About us

Built by students to make helping safer

AID-CARE-EVERYWHERE explains the world's biggest humanitarian crises in plain language, and points people who want to help to official, trusted places to give.

01Our goal

Turning concern into help that actually arrives

AID-CARE-EVERYWHERE is a website about the world's most serious humanitarian crises, from wars and famine to floods and earthquakes. We gather the latest information from trusted sources such as the United Nations and established aid organizations, and turn it into short, clear summaries that anyone can read in a few minutes.

Our second goal is just as important. When people decide they want to help, we point them only to official places to do it: the websites of recognized aid organizations, UN agencies and official government relief programs. Never copies, look-alikes or fundraisers we cannot check.

We do not collect money ourselves, and we never will. We are a guide, not a charity. Our job is to help you understand what is happening, and to make the path from caring to giving as short and as safe as possible.

What we do

  • Explain major crises in short, clear summaries
  • Show where every number comes from, with dates
  • Link only to official aid organizations and government sites

What we never do

  • Collect donations or payment details
  • Run fundraisers or crowdfunding pages
  • Recommend groups we cannot check

02Why it matters

When help goes missing, two people lose

As 2026 began, about 239 million people around the world needed humanitarian aid to survive and stay safe, according to the United Nations. At the same time, money for humanitarian work has fallen sharply, so every donation that reaches the right place matters more than ever.

Big emergencies also attract scammers. After disasters and wars, fake charities and fundraising pages appear quickly, often using names and photos that look just like the real thing. When someone gives to one of them, two people lose: the donor, who loses their money, and the family in crisis, who never receives the help that was meant for them.

Good intentions are not enough on their own. Knowing where to give, and how to check that an organization is real, is what turns kindness into real help. That is the gap this website tries to close.

03Who we are

Created by students who saw help go astray

AID-CARE-EVERYWHERE was created by a group of students. Like many people, we have watched friends, family members and neighbors try to help during a crisis, giving what they could to a cause that seemed real.

Too often, those donations ended up with scammers. The people giving lost their money, and the people who urgently needed help never received it. Seeing generosity wasted like this, on both sides, is what gave us the idea for this website.

For us, this project is a way to use what we are learning about technology to do something useful: to help people understand global crises, and to make sure that when they choose to help, their help goes where it is needed. We hope it makes giving a little safer for everyone who uses it.
