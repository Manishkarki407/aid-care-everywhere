Before you give

  • Check the web address matches the organization's official site.
  • Pay by credit card or check. Never by gift card, wire transfer or cryptocurrency.
  • Be wary of pressure to give right away, or of names that only sound like a real charity.
  • Report suspicious fundraisers to your national fraud-reporting service.
