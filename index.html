About us

Awareness first. Safe giving always.

A student-led project that turns humanitarian reports into clear, short stories, and points you to trusted ways to help.

More about the project

Our goal

Make major humanitarian crises easy to understand, and connect people who want to help with organizations already on the ground.

Why it matters

Needs are rising while humanitarian funding keeps falling. Knowing the facts is the first step to helping where it counts.

Giving safely

We never collect money. Every donate link goes to an organization's own official website, and we record when we last checked it.

How you can help

Help in three simple steps

  1. 1

    Learn the facts

    Read short, sourced summaries of what is happening and who is affected.

  2. 2

    Check the organization

    Use our safe-giving checklist to confirm a charity is real before you give.

  3. 3

    Give on the official site

    Every donate link opens the organization's own website, never a copy.
